# Contributing to Synapse OS

Thank you for considering a contribution to Synapse OS.

## Contributor terms are required

The official repository accepts contributions only under [`CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md`](CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md).

By submitting a pull request for inclusion and checking the CLA acknowledgement in the pull-request template, you affirm that you have read and agree to those contributor terms for the submitted Contribution.

If you do not agree to the contributor terms, do not submit material for inclusion. You may instead open an issue describing an idea in your own words without attaching code that you do not want licensed under the CLA.

## Ownership and third-party code

Only submit material you are authorized to contribute. Clearly identify:

- third-party source code;
- copied documentation or substantial excerpts;
- generated assets that carry restrictive terms;
- dependencies with notice or source-distribution obligations;
- employer-owned work or work subject to another agreement.

Do not remove existing copyright, license, provenance, or attribution notices.

## Licensing of accepted contributions

Accepted Contributions may be used, modified, commercialized, distributed, sublicensed, and relicensed by Cory Davis / NavisWORLD under the CLA. Contributors retain their own copyright ownership unless a separate written assignment says otherwise.

The public Synapse OS repository itself is distributed under the current root [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), with third-party components governed by their own licenses.

## Pull requests

A pull request should:

1. explain what changed and why;
2. include tests or verification appropriate to the change;
3. identify all third-party material introduced;
4. preserve provenance and license notices;
5. affirm the Contributor License Agreement checkbox.

A pull request that does not affirm the CLA may be reviewed for discussion but must not be merged into the official project until contributor rights are resolved.
